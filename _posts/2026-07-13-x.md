Today, I worked on several things on the game. First, I noticed that the gradients synced up weirdly on the tilemap. I fixed this by changing the tilemap shader to use world space instead of UVs. This fixed several issues: First, since tilemap UVs are broken into chunks, it results in weird lines in between chunks instead of a smooth gradient. Second, it meant that not all the blocks were perfectly in sync, and rather seemed like they were cut out of a bigger fabric.
