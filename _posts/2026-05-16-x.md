Over the last week, I worked on several things for my game. One big focus was working on the new level. For this level, I wanted to use basic concepts instead of focusing on gimmicks. I am pretty happy with the result. It is very difficult, but I don't think it is frustrating. After playtesting, I changed several parts to add more breaks and checkpoints in the middle of the level to help keep the player motivated.

I also made several small fixes. These include making it so that you can no longer jump on or interact with the spikes, which leads to less unintentional actions from the players and less exploits. I also changed the way the camera works. Previously, I was manually controlling the position when necessary. Now, I changed it so I adjust the offset of the camera to pan it when I don't want it strictly tracking the player. This leads to more natural transitions, but still isn't perfect. This is something I am going to continue to work on.
