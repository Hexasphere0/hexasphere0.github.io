Today, I have come up with a new ruleset I want to try. Here are the changes compared to the last game.
- One change will be removing materials from cards. Instead, there will be a energy deck and an extra deck. You get 1 action per turn, which you can spend to either play the top card of your energy deck, or play a card from your extra deck (the extra deck is not ordered, and you can access any card at any time). This gives the user an interesting choice to access utility or engine pieces in their extra deck at the cost of not ramping in resources. This will need a lot of balancing to ensure that it is a meaningful choice.
- Battle spirts cost & energy system.
- You start with 4 energy on the field.
- I am not sure if I want to allow the player to play any card in their extra deck. For now, to avoid adding additional cards, I will allow them to play any card this will probably change in the future). The limit on the extra deck will be 6 for now
- There is also a gauge zone, where you can put cards from any zone. Cards in the gauge zone can be used to pay for costs, then they go to the grave.
- A circle in the middle of the field that both players share. Both players have a core at their end of the circle that can be attacked. 
  ![[Pasted image 20260613190911.png|332]] 
- If a core is successfully attacked, you win the game. You can play creatures on your side of the field (red player can play on either of the 3 left squares, blue on the 3 right squares). Creatures can tap to move one square, also you can play creatures to an empty square behind squares you control. For example, if you are red and have a creature on the middle bottom square, you can play a creature to the bottom left square.

This idea did not work, because it just led to a bunch of board stalls and didn't really create interesting gameplay.
