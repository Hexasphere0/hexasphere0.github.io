Today, I worked on a new multiplayer level in my game. 

![[Pasted image 20260719213114.png]]

This is how it looks so far. My goal was to recreate my friends multiplayer level but with similar concepts, but better gameplay. My first step was to add checkpoints. While the idea of falling down is cool, I found it hard to make a level with my design style without spikes. I also tried to make it as easy as possible, because I think racing levels are fun when you are going quickly through them instead of struggling on one part. I also added several shortcuts which I really liked, because they are slightly faster but also slightly harder.
