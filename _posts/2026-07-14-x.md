Today, I started by refactoring the level management code. Basically, I needed to clean up the code and make it work with multiplayer. I started by creating a new checkpoint manager, which stores a list of checkpoints in the scene with their order, and a list of Booleans marking if those checkpoints are collected. As this exists on the player object, it works fine in multiplayer. The checkpoints can also call Collect Checkpoint during a collision by getting the component from the collider, making it a clean implementation. I also continued working on gameplay for my new level. I thought the difficulty spikes were too hard, so I wanted to gradually introduce concepts.
