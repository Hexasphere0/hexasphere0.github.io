Today in my card game, I improved several features. To start, I wanted to make cards more easily to understand. While the current frame is obviously a placeholder, I wrote some scripts in google sheets to automatically change the background and text color for the cards. I also changed the font for the numbers to make them easier to read.

![[Pasted image 20260607184052.png|283]]

Next, I made several major updates to help improve the gameplay. To start, I made it so the base material quality scales from 1-3, which will allow for more modularity in design. I also went through both decks to help smooth out the cost curves. A big problem in the last playtest was that many hands simply did not do anything. For example, if you had two cards in your ritual row with quality 2 and 3, as well as a card with cost 6 in your hand, you simply could not do anything. In this game, you need the material qualities to add up exactly to the cost of the card you are trying to play in order to play it. This led to many situations where the player simply could not do anything and had to wait to draw a card that fixed their hand. However, I felt like this was more of a deckbuilding issue then a core problem with the game. To fix this, I changed it so the water deck simply only played cards with cost 2, 3, or 5 and quality 2 or 3. This helps improve the math and overall consistency of the game. I also added several cards that can module the level, which will help make the dark deck feel better to play.

![[Pasted image 20260607184939.png|292]]

Finally, I also removed the entire resonator package from the dark deck. I felt like the cards felt way more restrained then I originally thought. They will definitely need reworks and/or more support to make them feel better.

I think the current gameplay of the water deck is interesting, but I wonder if the removal effects are too strong. This will need further testing.

Overall, I am pretty happy with this game system, and I'm excited to work on it more.
