- Added volley ability, which shoots multiple bullets when activated. The bullets move towards the mouse cursor, but have a random spread within a short angle. This is a good ability for dealing large amounts of damage to a boss, but it is hard to use against normal enemies due to the random spread.
- Added a melee slash ability, that deals damage to all units in a circle in front of the player. This is good for crowd control, as it can one shot normal enemies after a single damage upgrade. However, it's ineffective against ranged enemies and bosses as it requires you to get close to the target to attack.
