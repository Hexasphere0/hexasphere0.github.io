Today, me and my friend worked on a new level intended for racing in multiplayer.

![[Pasted image 20260715200401.png]]

The core idea is that if you make a mistake, instead of dying, you simply fall down. However, this level has MANY flaws, and ended up being quite a bad level. The first problem is the shortcuts. Because they involve spikes, they ended up being too much of a risk, and they are simply never worth it. Also, despite how the level looks, it ends up being super linear. I think it would be cooler if they were way more paths. Additionally, the gameplay was not super good. The middle section with the giant square was decent, but the part at the bottom was really bad. The part with a bunch of random blocks was okay, and the top part with the holes was plagued by physics issues. It's possible to get stuck in the middle of the colored section, and it feels super weird in general. If the physics was fixed, I think it could be fun. I think its also possible to loose too much progress, especially if you fall from the random blocks section. If this level was fixed, I think it would be fun.

In addition, I added a bunch of properties to the player that can be triggered in levels later. These include invulnerability and invisibility. I am currently working on a level that uses these.
