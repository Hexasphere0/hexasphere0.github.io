Today, I playtested my new card game for the first time. I played two games, with a few revisions in the middle. I think I learned a lot of things to improve the game for the future.

Each player plays this game on a board with 5 columns and two rows. The back row (ritual row) is used to play materials, and the front row (field row) is used to play creatures and spells.  Every card can be played as a material, with its respective elemental affinity (water, earth, dark, etc..) and its material quality (ranging from 1-3). To pay for costs, you have to have at least the required amount of affinity in the materials as well as matching its cost exactly with the combined quality of the materials used. Then, you place the materials under the card you played. If a creature with materials leaves the field, the materials go back into the ritual row if you have place. Spells simply stay on the field for a duration of time, and afterwards they are sent to the grave and return their materials to the ritual row.

I think the game did several things which were part of the design goals:
- Allowed for some amount of non-linear games. This can always be improved upon, but I think the core game system has potential to allow for this.
- Building big creatures felt fun.
- Running out of space on the field felt like a real concern. Cards that let you simply hold more cards on the board are very powerful. However, it might be too restrictive as decks get unlock more sophisticated combos. This will need further testing.
- Spells felt like they meshed well with creatures.
- ACAR (Any Card As a Resource) without feeling like an ACAR game. This is a very overused system but I think this system feels pretty different from the standard.
- Captured the feel of synchro summoning. (mechanic from Yugioh)

However, it missed many things, which I have fixed for the future.
- I wished there was more back and forth gameplay. I think moving away from hit points into single stat combat would fit the game better.
- Bane is an extremely strong keyword and needs to be more restricted. It allows you to gain an extreme amount of card advantage easily and punishes summoning creatures early. For example, if you summon a creature going second and your opponent played a creature with bane going first, you can get your materials stripped and exponentially fall behind. To fix this, I plan on making the cards with bane way more worse and restricted.
- Ritual circle is a card that allows you to draw a card every time you summon a creature. While this is a strong effect but sometimesbalanced, it is way too strong if you can get multiple on the field. To circumvent this, I changed the card so you can only have 1 on the field at a time.
- I want gaining card advantage to play bigger part. However, the easy access to bane restricted this, and there was not a big enough card pool to build engines.

There are also several things I want to reevaluate in the future, but I don't have enough experience with them yet:
- Is the current system where you can only attack in adjacent columns beneficial?
