Today, I worked on fixing several quality of life issues in my game. I started by writing a script to disable player collision. Most people seem to do this by changing the layer's physics properties, but that wasn't an option due to how were using layers for color changing. Instead, whenever a player spawns, it looks up every other player object and individually disables its collision with that player. This ended up working. I also wrote a script that worked pretty similarly to find other player objects and set their opacity to 20%. This required fixing the issue that when you changed colors, it changed the visual color of other players too. I still need to make it so changing colors actually synced over multiplayer, but now other players are grayed out to make them less distracting. Finally, I worked on improving the physics of the boost arrows a little bit, but they are going to need a major rework to feel as good as the rest of the game. I also cleaned up some extra code lying around before I did all the multiplayer reworks, which involved deleting the old level manager and the duplicate camera controller classes.
